# Faysal AI — Logo Kit

## Brand idea

**Two sides. One law. A clear decision.**

The open double ring represents balance and an active judicial process. The
central **F** makes the brand instantly recognizable. The cyan inner line
represents AI infrastructure; the restrained gold point represents a verified
final decision.

## Colors

| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight Navy | `#071D49` | Primary institutional color |
| Digital Cyan | `#00A6E8` | AI and digital infrastructure |
| Decision Gold | `#D8A21B` | Small verification/decision accent only |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Dark-background version |

## Usage

- Use `faysal-ai-logo-horizontal.svg` on white or light backgrounds.
- Use `faysal-ai-logo-horizontal-dark.svg` on navy or dark backgrounds.
- Use `faysal-ai-mark.svg` for app icons, avatars, seals and compact layouts.
- Use `faysal-ai-favicon.svg` for browser and PWA icons.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least half the width of the
  central **F**.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add shadows or enlarge the gold accent.
